Happy birthday, jackass.

I’m not great at this stuff, so I’ll keep it simple. It’s been eight months of us talking, and I’m still not bored of you, which is honestly impressive. When things got hard for me, you listened. You checked on me. You made me laugh when I didn’t feel like laughing, and you stayed around through all of it. You didn’t have to, and you did anyway. I won’t forget that.

Since then, I’m happier. I’m more open about things I never used to say out loud. When it’s just us, I feel like I can do anything, and that’s because of you. You’re different around me, and I love that I get to see it.

This year, I want more time with just us, more late night talks. I want us closer, and I want to back you in everything you’re going for, the way you’ve backed me.

I’m proud of you, more than I say. Your energy is something most people never get to see, and I get to. I trust you, and you make my life better just by being in it.

You’re my favorite person, and still my favorite jackass. Somehow that just happened one day and never stopped.

So here’s to you, the most unreasonably important jackass in my life. May your cake be enormous, your year be legendary, and may I be insufferable about how lucky I am. Now go eat cake.

Forever in your corner, jackass.
